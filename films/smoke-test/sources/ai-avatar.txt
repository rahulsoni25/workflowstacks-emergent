 Straight answers
 Questions founders ask about AI avatar videos.
 Can't I just buy the software myself? You can. A licence runs under AED 600 a month and you could have a twin by the weekend. The licence was never the hard part — writing scripts that sound like you, directing a capture that holds up in close-up, running Arabic and English from one approval, checking every lip-sync, and still shipping in the weeks you are too busy to think about it. That is where founders who buy the tool stop posting by week three.
 Will people know it's AI? Some will, and we label it to each platform's policy rather than hiding it. What holds attention is whether you said something worth hearing. We don't pretend you were in a studio, and we won't put words in your mouth that you haven't approved.
 What if ad prices go up? They move constantly — UAE Meta CPMs have swung by more than 2.5× inside a year. Your price is fixed for the term; what flexes is the reach we deliver, and we show you the auction data behind it. The media figure in a bundled package is a floor we spend, reported to the dirham.
 Who owns the avatar and the audience? You do. The twin is built from your likeness under a scoped consent, and paid campaigns run in your own ad account, so the pixel, the audiences and everyone who watched stay with you — including on the day you stop working with us.
 How much does an AI avatar video cost in Dubai? Production starts at AED 6,000 a month for 8 videos (AED 750 a video) and runs to AED 18,000 a month for 30 videos (AED 600 a video), excluding VAT, with a three-month minimum. Media budget is priced separately from AED 3,000 a month, or bundled with production from AED 11,000. For comparison, a three-day shoot in Dubai costs AED 35,000–60,000 and buys one day of footage in one language.
 How long does it take to build an AI avatar? Seven days from the capture session to an approved twin, with your first four videos scheduled. The capture itself takes 45 minutes, and we record your voice in both languages in the same sitting, so there is no second booking.
 Do you make AI avatar videos in Arabic? Yes. The Authority and Omnipresence plans produce every video in English and Arabic from one approval, with no reshoot. Presence is a single-language plan.
 Which platforms do the videos go on? Vertical, captioned videos are posted on LinkedIn and Instagram first. Paid amplification, when you take a media plan, runs on Instagram Reels and Stories, Meta and YouTube, and on LinkedIn targeted by job title.
 Is this the same as an AI clone or AI spokesperson video? In substance, yes. AI clone, AI twin, digital twin and AI spokesperson all describe a video presenter generated from a real person's face and voice, which is what we build. The difference is the service around it: we direct the capture, write the scripts, run English and Arabic from one approval, check every lip-sync and publish on a schedule, rather than handing you a software licence.
 Where is Founder Avatar Studio based? FluoDigital is an AI video agency with studios in Dubai Silicon Oasis and Mumbai, and works with founders in both cities. Enquiries are answered the same working day, from Dubai, on WhatsApp, phone or email.
