01 · The brief

A national star, a regional market.

The brief covered seven areas: audience demographics, comment language and sentiment, peer benchmarks, film buzz, Tamil film reception, press and a cross-platform check.

Audience

City, state, age, gender and language of her followers.

Conversation

What language people comment in, and how they feel.

Benchmarks

How she ranks against 10 comparable actresses and in monthly popularity lists.

03 · Findings

Reach is national. Relevance in Tamil Nadu is thin.

Key findings from the assessment.

< 2.8%

Chennai share of her audience

Chennai is not in her top-10 audience cities; Tamil is not in her top-10 languages (< 0.37%).

~5%

Tamil Nadu share of her pan-India film’s gross

Her only multi-language release earned little in Tamil Nadu.

1

Tamil-script comment in 16,311

Comment language showed almost no Tamil-speaking audience.

Bottom lineA national reach face. For Tamil Nadu, pair her with a Tamil co-face, use Tamil-language creative and avoid cultural cues that had drawn recent backlash.

04 · What it teaches

Three takeaways

Check the state, not the follower count

A large national following can still be nearly absent in one state.

Read the comments

Comment language is a fast, honest signal of who is really listening.

Show the proof

Every claim shipped with a screenshot, so the client could check it themselves.
