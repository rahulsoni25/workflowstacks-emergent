01 · The brief

City cans need city stories. Which ones?

The brief asked six questions: market territories, city pride, conversation owners, category UGC, “My City” UGC and an influencer shortlist.

No assumed topics

Territories had to come from what people in each market actually post.

Organic, not paid

Brand-paid content would distort the picture, so it had to come out first.

Influencers verified

A circulated shortlist needed checking before any budget went to it.

02 · What we did

A custom measurement pipeline, not a tool export.

Every engagement figure is a real number from a real post, and every post can be traced to its URL.

  1. 01
    245 discovery queriesEnglish, Hindi, Punjabi and transliterated terms across 4 markets × 15 territories.
  2. 02
    Scrape and clean1,847 Reels scraped (98.3% of URLs found), deduplicated and classified.
  3. 03
    Remove paid content414 declared paid partnerships (22.4%) excluded before any ranking.
  4. 04
    Rank by volume, engagement and viralityEach territory tiered per market; thin cells marked “Insufficient N”, never force-ranked.
  5. 05
    Audit the influencer listEvery handle checked against its live profile.

Bottom lineCreative territories differ sharply by market, so one national city-can story would miss most of them. And verification matters: 40% of a circulated influencer list did not exist.

04 · What it teaches

Three takeaways

Rank, don’t guess

Choosing territories is a ranking decision; 311–416 posts per market separate top tiers from bottom ones.

Strip paid first

One in five Reels was paid; leaving them in would have skewed every table.

Verify every handle

Checking influencers one by one saved budget from fake or inflated accounts.
