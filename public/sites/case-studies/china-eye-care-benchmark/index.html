04 · What it teaches

Three takeaways

Own the explanation

Consumers know symptoms, not mechanisms; a science brand can own that layer.

Professional authority converts

Rx badging, pharmacist 1-to-1, approval numbers and doctor explainers.

One-number science

A single engineering metric, repeated everywhere, beats long claim lists.
