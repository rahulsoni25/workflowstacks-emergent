01 · The brief

A paid entry fee needs trust before it converts.

The goal is the maximum number of genuine registrations. The fee filters out casual clicks, so the funnel has to build belief first and ask for payment second.

Scattered audience

Women aged 18+ anywhere in India, married or unmarried, across metros and smaller cities.

A paid decision

Registration is paid and non-refundable, so the funnel plans for more than one touch before the ask to pay.

Genuine, not just cheap

Low-cost leads only matter if they turn into real paid entries.

02 · What we did

A full funnel, tested audience by audience.

Every campaign is named, measured and compared, so budget moves to the audiences that register at the lowest cost.

  1. 01
    Top of funnel: reach and followsTraffic and Instagram-follower campaigns: 37,817 link clicks at ₹0.32 each for the pageant’s Instagram push.
  2. 02
    Middle: website leadsLead campaigns on the landing page at ₹130 to ₹205 per website lead, building the pool we later convert.
  3. 03
    Bottom: registration salesSales campaigns optimised to the Meta pixel purchase event (the paid registration), across age bands, interests, metros, affluent pin codes and Advantage+.
  4. 04
    RemarketingSite visitors and leads are retargeted with registration campaigns, so warm prospects get the ask to pay.
  5. 05
    Next seasonNew teaser creative on Meta, with Google Search Ads alongside.

04 · What it teaches

Three takeaways

Warm beats cold

Remarketing delivered the cheapest paid registrations in the account.

Go broad on age, not narrow on interest

Age 30–54 and 40+ broad campaigns beat interest and city targeting on cost per registration.

Buy leads to feed conversion

₹130–205 leads are cheap on their own and valuable as a remarketing audience.
