01 · The brief

The liver doesn't complain. So nobody listens.

#LiverKiSuno (“listen to your liver”) is Zydus Lifesciences' public-awareness platform for early attention to liver health. The job: turn a silent organ into a conversation India actually has — and send people to the Ayushman Liver and LiverForever websites.

Make it mass

Reach India at scale, with a planned delivery of 595 Mn impressions, 580 Mn video views and 140 Mn engagements.

Make it credible

Health messaging needs authority — doctors, specialists and a trusted news platform, not just a celebrity.

Make it precise

Pair mass reach with focused cohorts: healthcare professionals, premium neighbourhoods and affluent metro audiences.

“Sometimes, the real villains may be within us.”Campaign line · #LiverKiSuno creative

03 · How we did it

Mass reach, engineered.

Reach alone is easy to buy. The work was in deciding who sees what, where, and proving it every week.

Cohort P1 · Facebook

Doctors & HCPs

Seeded with doctors, dentists, pharmacists, nurses and allied healthcare professionals pan-India, then scaled with Meta Advantage+ audience expansion once the seed was saturated.

Cohort P2 · Instagram

Premium neighbourhoods

Premium micro-markets in Mumbai, Ahmedabad and other metros, ages 24–65 — run at the highest frequency of the three (2.44).

Cohort P3 · Instagram

Affluent metro

iOS users, frequent international travellers and premium-goods buyers in metro regions, ages 24–65 — 472.9 Mn impressions.

  1. 01
    Full-funnel platform mixMeta (Facebook + Instagram), YouTube and Connected TV, Google Display and Performance Max, and LinkedIn — plus Zepto, Inshorts, print and trade media.
  2. 02
    City-level targetingGoogle flights targeted Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kolkata, layered on state-level Meta delivery across 35 states.
  3. 03
    Content sequenced as dropsFilms, ABP News episodes and podcast episodes released as a series of drops, each with its own paid push, with hygiene content holding the gaps.
  4. 04
    Two destinations, one measurement viewGA4 on both brand websites — Ayushman Liver and LiverForever — reported alongside media delivery in a weekly campaign report.

05 · What we learned

Three takeaways for health brands.

Authority outreaches celebrity

The ABP News expert series delivered the most reach of any content type — 326.7 Mn, ahead of the brand films' 210.0 Mn.

Celebrity drives engagement

The three Ashish Vidyarthi films earned 140.1 Mn engagements — the most of any content type, from fewer pieces.

Women listen just as closely

Women were 19% of reach but engaged at almost the same rate as men (23.0% vs 24.3%) — room to grow reach without losing attention.
