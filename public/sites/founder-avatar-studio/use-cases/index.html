Use case 01 · Auto repairing workshop · Al Quoz, Dubai

The founder tells customers why to choose his workshop, in his own face and voice.

Al Meeraj Auto Repairing Workshop is in Al Quoz, Dubai, and its founder is D.K. Singh. Most of what a workshop explains, it explains again and again. His AI digital twin says it on video, in two films: why people choose Al Meeraj, and what "your complete car care centre" covers. Both keep the workshop's number on screen from start to finish, so a viewer can call straight from the video.

In the two videos

Why people choose Al Meeraj

Experience (26 years of automotive repair), honest repairs, a warranty of up to 25,000 km or 2 years, and a courtesy car while yours is being repaired.

Your complete car care centre

All makes and models (European, American, Chinese and Japanese), body and paint, detailing and coatings, wheels and upholstery.

Use case 02 · Handcrafted edible blooms · Dubai

Gifts sell on occasions, and every occasion deserves its own film.

Bougie Bloom (bougiebloom.ae) makes handcrafted edible blooms from 100% Belgian couverture chocolate. In this festive brand film, an AI digital twin presents the Bougie Bloom box in a candlelit hall, close-ups of the blooms follow, and it signs off on the brand line. With a twin as the presenter, the film for the next occasion is a new script rather than a new shoot.

In the film

The presenter

Introduces the Bougie Bloom box in a candlelit, festive hall.

The blooms

Close-ups, captioned "Handcrafted edible blooms."

The proof

"100% Belgian couverture chocolate."

The sign-off

"Beautifully crafted. Thoughtfully gifted." and bougiebloom.ae.

Use case 03 · Restaurant · Dubai

The owner introduces what's new on the menu, without stepping away from service.

Mavericks Foodie House is a restaurant in Dubai. People choose where to eat by the people behind it as much as by the plates, and a restaurant has something new to say most weeks: a dish, a special, a change of hours. Each is worth a video from the owner, and each is needed this week, not next month. The twin keeps pace with the menu, so the restaurant can post as often as the menu changes.

What the twin makes

Dish of the week

What it is, why it's on, and who will like it.

Specials and events

Weekend offers, set menus and nights worth booking for.

Hours and seasons

Changed timings and festive menus, announced clearly before the day.

The story of the place

Why it exists and what it cares about, told by the owner.

Use case 04 · Insurance · Investments · Will · HM360°

The founder explains a whole financial plan in under a minute, in his own face and voice.

Hermith Mana is the founder of HM360°, whose line is "Winning from every angle". His AI digital twin presents a 57-second film that asks one question, "Are there gaps between your policies?", walks through everything HM360° covers and argues for one joined-up plan. It closes with a plain disclaimer (not financial advice; investments carry risk) and labels the avatar as AI-generated. The same film is cut twice: widescreen, and 9:16 for WhatsApp.

In the film

The question

"Are there gaps between your policies?" and "Don't just cover one side."

What HM360° covers

Protection, wealth and future, group cover, and will and legacy planning.

The point

"30+ years. I've seen people let down… not by bad products, but by the gaps between their policies."

The promise

"One plan. No jargon. No pressure." Then WhatsApp and hm360degree.com.
