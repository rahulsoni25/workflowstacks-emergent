Straight answers

The questions founders actually ask.

Can't I just buy the software myself?

You can. A licence runs under AED 600 a month and you could have a twin by the weekend. The licence was never the hard part — writing scripts that sound like you, directing a capture that holds up in close-up, running Arabic and English from one approval, checking every lip-sync, and still shipping in the weeks you are too busy to think about it. That is where founders who buy the tool stop posting by week three.

Will people know it's AI?

Some will, and we label it to each platform's policy rather than hiding it. What holds attention is whether you said something worth hearing. We don't pretend you were in a studio, and we won't put words in your mouth that you haven't approved.

What if ad prices go up?

They move constantly — UAE Meta CPMs have swung by more than 2.5× inside a year. Your price is fixed for the term; what flexes is the reach we deliver, and we show you the auction data behind it. The media figure in a bundled package is a floor we spend, reported to the dirham.

Who owns the avatar and the audience?

You do. The twin is built from your likeness under a scoped consent, and paid campaigns run in your own ad account, so the pixel, the audiences and everyone who watched stay with you — including on the day you stop working with us.
