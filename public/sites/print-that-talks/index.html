How it works

Four steps. You only do the first.

1 · Tell us what to say

Send your own video, or your photos and a few lines about what you want people to hear. For a digital twin, give us one 45-minute session.

2 · We make and host it

Our in-house editors produce the video, put it on a mobile page in your brand colours, and send you a print-ready QR code file.

3 · You print it

Add the QR code to your cards, menus, boxes or banners, with your own printer or one of our partner print studios.

4 · They scan, you see

Customers point their camera and the video plays. You get a scan report every month, and can swap the video whenever your offer changes.

Questions

Before you print.

Do my customers need an app?

No. They point their phone camera at the QR code and the video opens in the browser.

Can I change the video after printing?

Yes. The QR code points to your video page, not to a fixed file, so we can swap the video for a new offer or season and nothing needs reprinting.

Do I pay for every printed piece?

No. You pay per video. The same QR code can go on 50 business cards or 5,000 boxes.

How small can the QR code be?

We send a print-ready file and test every code before it goes to print. As a rule, keep it at least 2 × 2 cm on a plain, high-contrast background.

Who owns the video?

You do. For a digital twin, you sign a consent form first, approve every script and video, and the twin is deleted if you stop working with us.

How long does the video stay online?

12 months of hosting is included. After that it renews for AED 149 a year per QR code, or you can take the video file and stop.

Can it be in Arabic?

Yes. Any video can have an Arabic version, and with the Twin level your twin speaks both English and Arabic from one approval.
