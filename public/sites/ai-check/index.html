          Why check

          AI answers sound sure. That doesn't make them right.

          More people now ask an assistant before they search, hire, buy or meet someone. It's worth knowing what those answers say — about you, and about the things you're deciding on.

          Mix-ups

          Ask about a person and an assistant can blend two people with the same name, or fill gaps with guesses. Checking is the only way to know.

          Disagreement

          The same question in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google can give four different shortlists. Where they agree is worth a closer look; where they don't, check the sources.

          Change

          Answers shift with wording, location and time. A check today is a snapshot, so it's worth repeating.

          Straight answers

          Questions about AI Check.

          Is AI Check free?

          Yes. No sign-up, no account and no limit. It runs in your browser.

          Do you see what I type?

          No. Your name, your question and your marks stay in your browser, saved only on your device so a refresh doesn't lose them. Nothing is sent to us. The assistants you open see the question you send them, under their own privacy policies.

          Why do I open each AI myself?

          So you see the real answer from the real product, in your own account, rather than a copy. It also means the tool costs nothing to run, which is why it is free.

          Which AI assistants does it use?

          ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google (its AI Overview, when Google shows one). ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google open with the question filled in; for Gemini, paste the copied question.

          What can I do if AI says something wrong about me?

          Mark it Wrong so you can see how often it happens, then use the feedback button on that answer — each assistant has one. Describing yourself clearly and consistently on your own profiles gives assistants better material to work from, though nobody can guarantee what they will say.

          Why do the AIs give different answers?

          They are built differently, search the web differently or not at all, and draw on different sources. That is why comparing them is useful: agreement is a signal, disagreement is a reason to check the sources.

          Does it work on my phone?

          Yes. Each assistant opens in a new tab, or in its app if you have it. Come back to this tab to mark the answer; your marks are kept.
