        What is an AI visibility audit?

        An AI visibility audit checks whether AI assistants recommend your business when buyers ask them for one. We put the questions your buyers ask to ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, record which businesses each answer names and which websites it cites, and show you where you are missing and why.

        How is it different from an SEO audit?

        An SEO audit asks where your pages rank in Google's results. An AI visibility audit asks whether an assistant names you in its answer at all. The two overlap, since both depend on your website, your business listings and your reviews, but a business can rank well on Google and still be left out of AI answers, and the fixes are not always the same.

        Which AI assistants do you check?

        ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. The free scan on this page opens the same four, so you can see the answers for yourself.

        Why does the free scan have me run the prompts myself?

        So you see the real answers in the real products rather than our summary of them. The scan writes the prompts and opens each assistant for you; you mark whether you were named. It runs in your browser, and nothing reaches us unless you choose to send it.

        Will everyone see the same AI answer?

        No. Answers change with the wording of the question, the person's location and history, the day, and whether the assistant searched the web. One answer is a snapshot, which is why the report runs 25 prompts across four assistants rather than one, and why the score is worth re-checking over time.

        Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

        No, and be wary of anyone who does. Nobody controls what an AI assistant says. What can be changed is what it draws on: your website and its structured data, your business listings, your reviews, and the third-party pages it cites. The 90-day plan works on those, in priority order.

        What are GEO and AEO?

        Generative engine optimisation (GEO) and answer engine optimisation (AEO) are two names for the same work: making a business easier for AI assistants and answer engines to find, understand and cite. An AI visibility audit is where that work starts, because it shows what to fix first.
